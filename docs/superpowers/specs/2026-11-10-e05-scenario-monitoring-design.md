# Phase 2b: ANA-E05 scenario and monitoring workflow — design

Date: 2026-11-10. Issue: thaiphi/well-injection-insights #6 (PRD `docs/project-2-swd-injection-analytics-expansion-prd.md` §4, ANA-E05). Status: owner-approved in conversation, part by part, on 2026-11-10, including the Part B scope (option 2); this document awaits the owner's approval as a whole. Design advice on parts 2 and 3 came from an independent Fable review; the owner accepted its recommendations.

## Why

The connected disposal case (`swd-injection-analytics/src/disposalCase.ts`) already does most of ANA-E05:
- monthly demand against the wells' capacity envelopes;
- availability applied once;
- proportional allocation and unmet volume;
- the required surface and bottomhole pressure for each allocation;
- a base case against one changed well (J multiplier, tubing ID, availability);
- refusing an export whose inputs changed.

Three things are missing:
1. A plain-language reading of the results.
2. A way to see whether a well's reported past injection makes its forecast share believable.
3. A way to ask "what if all my wells take water worse (or better) than assumed?". Today only one well and one setting can change at a time.

## Goals

The three parts, all inside the existing **Disposal capacity case** workspace:
- **Part A, Case summary:** short sentences generated by fixed rules from the calculated case.
- **Part B, History vs forecast:** a well-by-well comparison of reported H-10 injection with the case's forecast allocation.
- **Part C, Well-performance cases:** Pessimistic / Base / Optimistic cases that change every well together, side by side, with factor attribution.

The summary also reports on Parts B and C.

## Non-goals

- No change to the calculation engine (`buildDisposalCase`); all three parts consume its results.
- No demand uncertainty. The owner chose well performance as the uncertainty that matters.
- No new navigation tab or dashboard (D19).
- No LLM text.
- No saved cases (D10).

## Anti-goals (a failure even if it works)

The feature fails if any of the following happens:
- A sentence, chart or export implies proven, sustainable, calibrated or safe capacity, or a compliance, permit or operating finding.
- A missing month or missing history is counted as zero.
- A pressure agreement is presented as validating the model.
- A pre-filled pessimistic or optimistic value reads as a typical range.
- The work pre-empts #7 (transient buildup) or #8 (multiwell interference).

## Constraints

- **Determinism:** the same inputs give byte-identical results, summary text and exports. Editing any input invalidates the displayed summary, comparisons and exports, as the disposal case already does.
- **Availability** is applied exactly once. Parts B and C only change `well.availability` and let the engine apply it.
- **Labels:** reported, synthetic, assumed and calculated values stay distinct (E01, D12). Every case value that Part C changes is labelled *assumed*.
- **Pressure basis:** every pressure record declares psig or psia (D8). A comparison without a confirmed basis is not made; the sentence reads "pressure not checked".
- **Data:** only synthetic or fictional values in tests and evidence (fictional UIC `000000417`). No Explorer artifact is copied into the repo.
- **Preservation:** one reviewed layer, `phase-2b-scenario-monitoring`, covers every protected file changed and every new file pinned (skill: `.claude/skills/preservation-layer`).
- **Checks:** direct Node paths only.

## Part A: Case summary

**Reader:** the person using the app, on screen next to the results. It is short; numbers are available on request.

**Placement:** directly under the disposal case results.

**Lines:** one line per rule, in this order.

| Rule | Example output |
|---|---|
| A1 Fit | "All demand is placed in every month." / "Unplaced water starts in 2026-08: 41,200 bbl over 5 months." |
| A2 Limits | "Well A is limited by its bottomhole pressure limit; Well B by its operating ceiling." |
| A3 Pressure warnings | "2026-09, Well B: the allocated rate needs a surface pressure the model does not support (reason)." |
| A4 Tightest month | "Least spare capacity: 2026-07, 1,900 bbl." |
| A5 Compared case | "The compared case (Well A, J × 0.8) moves the first shortfall from 2026-10 to 2026-08 and adds 18,000 bbl unplaced." |
| A6 History (Part B) | One line per well: "within reported history", "not shown in reported history" or "cannot be checked". |
| A7 Cases (Part C) | "Pessimistic: water stops fitting in 2026-05 (base 2026-08), 96,000 bbl unplaced." |
| A8 Closing (fixed) | "Screening estimate from assumed inputs; not proven field capacity." |

**Behaviour:**
- Each line can expand to list the input values it used and the method it relies on, quoting the case's existing `methods` entries (capacity, allocation and so on). No new arithmetic is introduced except comparisons and sums of results the case already holds.
- **No advice:** no "drill a well", "raise the limit" or "acidize".
- **Downloads:** the same lines, with their cited values, go into the CSV and JSON downloads, so a saved file matches the screen.
- **Stale results:** the summary is discarded with the results when an input changes.

## Part B: History vs forecast

**Purpose (the owner's words):** "whether the past injection makes the forecast believable".

**Where history exists (owner decision, 2026-11-10).** History is checked wherever a case well already has a linked H-10 package. Today that is only the **sample case**:
- It holds two TRRC injectors with their Explorer records.
- It links them by stable ID in `sampleSession.selections[]` (`wellId` → artifact).
- Uploaded (`user`) and `demo` cases read "cannot be checked: no H-10 history loaded" for every well.
- An Explorer hand-off alone (`public`) still opens no disposal case (`web/disposal-context.mjs`).

Connecting Explorer packages to the user's own cases is **out of E05**: it becomes the first step of #9 (ANA-E10), which loads several single-UIC packages (D14). Part B is written so #9 only has to supply the same `wellId` → package link. There is no matching by display name.

**History set.** These are the months included by the user's H-10 diagnostics decisions (`h10Diagnostics.ts`, decisions and `coverage()`), as non-gap months with a reported rate above 0.

**Rules:**

- **V1, rate (the main check).** Compare the forecast calendar-average allocated rate (`allocations[].averageStbd`, STB/day) with the highest reported monthly `rateBpd`. Reported bbl/day is treated as STB/day and labelled so; bbl/month is shown for display only.
  - Ratio ≤ 1: "within reported history".
  - Ratio > 1: "N× the highest reported month (value, month); not shown in reported history".
  - Also state how many history months reached the forecast rate.
- **V2, onstream rate.** When availability < 1, state the onstream rate and that H-10 monthly data cannot show it.
- **P1, pressure (a warning only, never a pass).** This check runs only with a confirmed surface basis (Well assumptions, `wellAssumptions.ts`):
  - Compare the case's `requiredSurfacePsia` with the highest reported maximum pressure, converted with `toAbsolute`.
  - If the requirement is higher: "requires more surface pressure than any reported month".
  - If it is lower, nothing positive is said; only the rate-mismatch caveat is shown.
  - If no basis is confirmed: "pressure not checked", with the existing basis prompt. There is no fallback (D8).
- **Three different empty states, never zero:**
  - no package or no included months: "cannot be checked";
  - reported zero injection: "no injection reported";
  - missing months: a gap, never zero.

**Display.** One chart per mapped well, on one timeline: reported monthly volume (labelled *reported*), then forecast allocated volume (labelled *forecast*), with a marked boundary. The chart is not thinned, and gaps are kept (D16).

**Wording.** Use only "shown / not shown in reported history". Never say proven, sustainable, calibrated or validated, and draw no inference about reservoir pressure, J or damage.

## Part C: Well-performance cases

**Cases:** Pessimistic, Base and Optimistic, side by side, against the same demand.

**Factors.** Each factor applies to every well:
- J multiplier;
- reservoir-pressure offset, in psi on each well's declared datum, in psia;
- availability.

**Rules:**
- **Flat, not time-varying.** Each case is labelled "end-of-period condition held for every month". A ramp would read as a buildup forecast, which belongs to #7.
- **No defaults.** Pessimistic and Optimistic start equal to Base (×1, +0 psi, the same availability). The user types every value. Every value is labelled *assumed well-performance case*. No probabilities and no P10/P90.
- **Headroom.** Show each well's room under the limit (bottomhole limit − reservoir pressure) and the offset as a percentage of it.
  - If the offset uses up all the room, report "no headroom" for that well; the engine already returns zero capacity there.
  - Inputs outside the engine's 100–10,000 psia bounds are blocked with the engine's message.
- **Attribution.** Also run each factor alone. Report the unplaced volume per factor, labelled non-additive.
- **Results per case:**
  - first month that water doesn't fit;
  - total unplaced volume;
  - the limiting constraint per well;
  - the unsupported-pressure months.
- **Fluid reference pressure** is held unchanged; the panel says so.

**Engine use.** A new pure module, following the `disposalSensitivity.ts` pattern:
- clone the case input;
- change all wells;
- set a neutral comparison;
- call `buildDisposalCase`;
- keep `.base`.

`summarize` in `disposalSensitivity.ts` is exported and reused rather than copied.

## Calls made after the builder check (Main's calls, for owner review)

- **Part B months and basis.** For the sample case, the disposal panel does not depend on the live, single-well H-10 diagnostics session, which is discarded when the selected well changes.
  - For each mapped well, it recomputes the default month inclusions with the existing diagnostics rules (`diagnoseMonthly`) and labels them "default H-10 month inclusions".
  - It asks for that well's surface pressure basis inside the panel.
  - Changing a mapping or a basis invalidates the summary and exports.
- **Part B comparison point.** The check runs per forecast month on the **Base** scenario only.
  - The headline uses the forecast month with the highest allocated rate, with the count of months above the reported peak.
  - The pressure warning uses the month with the highest required surface pressure.
- **Part C availability** is a multiplier on each well's own availability, capped at 1 and labelled "capped" where the cap applies. Wells keep their differences. Base is ×1.

## Kept out of E05

- **Belongs to #7 (ANA-E06):**
  - reservoir pressure calculated from cumulative injection, rate history, time or rock properties;
  - any buildup curve;
  - skin or wellbore radius. The J multiplier is never called skin.
- **Belongs to #9 (ANA-E10):**
  - loading Explorer packages alongside a user or demo case, and linking them to case wells.
- **Belongs to #8:**
  - effects of offset wells, well-to-well coupling and distances. The uniform pressure offset is a declared assumption, not interference.
- **Optional extra:** a reservoir-pressure parameter in the single-well sweep. It is not required for E05; the builder may add it only if it is trivial and tested.

## Acceptance (from issue #6)

| Criterion | How it is shown |
|---|---|
| Cases rerun deterministically | Byte-identical summary, Part B, Part C and exports across reruns (unit tests) |
| Availability applied once | Part C availability-only case equals the engine's result; zero availability contributes zero (unit tests) |
| Edits invalidate exports | Browser: edit an input after a result; the summary, panels and downloads are withdrawn |
| Synthetic scenarios retain labels | Exports and screen carry reported / forecast / assumed / calculated labels (tests and browser) |
| No future field capacity presented as proven | Fixed closing line; a wording test bans "proven", "sustainable", "calibrated", "validated" and "safe" in generated text |

**Also required:**
- missing history gives "cannot be checked", not zero;
- a well without a confirmed basis gives "pressure not checked";
- a no-headroom well is reported;
- the full gate passes (`node scripts/check-all.mjs`): app tests, 248 carried oracles, `tsc`, preservation;
- browser evidence at desktop and mobile widths (skill `browser-check`), recorded in `demo/E05-SCENARIO-MONITORING-VERIFICATION.md`;
- independent review (agent `reviewer`), then QA (agent `qa`), then the owner accepts the layer.

## Left to the builder (plan)

- The list of protected files touched (likely `web/disposal-case.mjs`, `src/disposalSensitivity.ts`, `web/index.html`, and possibly `src/disposalCase.ts` only for the CSV writer).
  - Keep the list minimal.
  - `web/import-ui.mjs` keeps its 4 CR bytes if touched.
- The exact sentence templates, within the examples and banned words above.
- Whether Part B's charts and Part C's table go in the existing SVG export or a new one. Either way, exports are generated from result objects and re-verified before download.
- Build order: A, then B, then C. Each part is testable alone, and the summary rules for B and C (A6, A7) land with their parts.
