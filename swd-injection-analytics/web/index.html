2. Fluid assumptions

The pyResToolbox brine adaptation supplies Bw, density and viscosity at the reference condition, held uniform throughout the pipe. NaCl mass percent is not TDS mg/L.

Declare surface and bottomhole temperatures. The prescribed temperature profile is linear with vertical depth; straight deviation uses MD/TVD. Local pressure changes Bw, density and viscosity. This gas-free screening model excludes heat transfer, fittings, pumps and multiphase flow; unsupported conditions block or flag results.

What data do I need?

History: confirmed produced-water rates, producer identities and training cutoff; optional schedules for distinct new producers.

Each well: stock-tank J, reservoir and bottomhole limiting pressures at the same declared datum, operating ceiling and availability. Then enter MD/TVD measured down from the wellhead to that datum, tubing ID, roughness and a separate absolute surface limit.

Fluid: reference absolute pressure, temperature and NaCl composition. The demonstration supplies illustrative assumptions. User geometry starts blank and must be declared.

No permit limit, public monthly pressure or guessed geometry becomes a calibrated engineering input.

Water / brine PVT

Explore pressure-dependent fluid properties using an attributed pyResToolbox browser adaptation.

Independent assumed fluid case. The active dataset above stays selected, but supplies none of these inputs. These sweep results are independent of injection capacity and reported RRC pressures. In Capacity scenarios, choose the depth-dependent brine model to calculate local fluid properties, wellhead pressure and their effect on constrained disposal capacity.

Fluid assumptions

Absolute pressure: 100–10,000 psia · 60–250 °F · 0–20 NaCl wt% · 2–101 points. Bounded demonstration limits.

What data do I need?

Supply fluid temperature, absolute pressure range and NaCl mass percent. Without these measurements, the entered values remain assumptions.

RRC availability: monthly reported pressure has an unspecified basis. It does not supply these absolute fluid pressures, temperature or NaCl composition.

Method, evidence & limitations

Gas-free NaCl liquid (methane saturation = 0). IAPWS-IF97 Region 1 freshwater density with the source Spivey salt correction. Bw is source standard density / fluid density. Viscosity and compressibility follow the source brine equations.

Compressibility is the source modified Spivey result, not the derivative of the IAPWS-adjusted density. NaCl wt% is not mg/L TDS. No mixed-ion, CO₂, methane-saturated, transient or wellbore model is included.

Source standard: 15 °C (59 °F); freshwater 0.1013 MPa and saline standard integral 0.1015 MPa. Original viscosity temperature offset (273 K) retained.

45 direct local-library reference cases cover three temperatures, three salt concentrations and five pressures, checking Bw, density, viscosity and compressibility. This establishes implementation agreement, not field calibration or the full correlation domain.

Exact local source snapshot retained; package metadata says 2.5.0 and does not establish an unmodified published release. The browser does not call Python or a live MCP service.

Acceptance blockers — full acceptance remains open

Existing-producer demand, spreadsheet errors, finite-result guards and exhibit consistency have focused checks. Failed calculations withhold their affected outputs while independent analyses remain usable. Technical CSV, SVG and PDF verify the exhibit content hash; this establishes consistency, not authorship. Legacy forecasts without raw training history remain explicitly unverified.

  • The live brine reference now uses the source-checked PVT method with explicit NaCl mass units. Retained historical Nexus artifacts preserve their original method; its incorrect Bw pressure trend is not corrected or endorsed by the carried tolerance.
  • STORED and compressed XLSX payloads have CRC and expanded-size checks before workbook parsing; original upload bytes and hashes are preserved. Native decompression does not prove complete trailing-byte consumption or a hard memory ceiling. Comprehensive decompression/dependency review remains open.
  • The cited steady radial example (A6), depth-dependent gas-free brine wellbore (SWD-10) and one-at-a-time capacity sweeps (SWD-12) have bounded calculation and export evidence. They remain engineering screening, not sustainable-capacity or calibrated-response forecasts. Transient/layered/calibrated models (SWD-11), comprehensive parser review and two-app acceptance remain open.

Scenario assumptions & interpretation

Two fictional injectors share a formation pressure of 2,000 psia and an assumed bottomhole limit of 3,500 psia at the same formation reference depth. Expansion adds available capacity after downtime. New producer demand starts at 6,000 STB/d and declines about 2.5% per month.

Read the case

Capacity is the smaller of pressure-limited and operating-ceiling rates, multiplied by availability. No real-well operating recommendation is implied.
